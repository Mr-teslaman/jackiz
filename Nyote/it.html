Introduction to IT Support in Kenya

IT Support in Kenya involves maintaining, managing, and troubleshooting systems and networks that power our businesses and organizations. Whether you're starting or already in the field, this guide will help you understand how IT support works in Kenyan environments.

Core IT Support Services

Typical IT services include network setup, user support, cybersecurity, data backup, and hardware maintenance. Kenyan companies rely on these services to keep operations running smoothly and securely.

Essential Tools and Software

From remote support software like AnyDesk and TeamViewer to management tools like Zendesk and Microsoft Admin Center, Kenyan IT support professionals use reliable tools to deliver excellent service.

Training and Certifications

Build your skills through CompTIA A+, Cisco CCNA, and Google IT Support certifications. Many local institutions in Kenya offer IT training — including KCA University, Zetech, and Strathmore.

Contact & Community

Join Kenya’s growing IT community. Collaborate, share solutions, and find job opportunities through networks like LinkedIn and local tech meetups.
