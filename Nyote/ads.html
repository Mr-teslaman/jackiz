Overview

What Google Ads is and when to use each campaign type

Search Ads

Text ads shown in Google Search results. Great for capturing intent (high purchase/buying signals).

Display Ads

Visual ads across websites and apps — useful for awareness and retargeting.

Shopping & Video

Shopping ads for e-commerce and YouTube video ads for brand storytelling.

Step-by-step Setup (Beginner)

Create your first campaign and track results

1. Account basics

Link your Google Ads to Google Analytics, set billing, and verify conversion tracking (events or goals).

2. Campaign goal & type

Choose goals (Sales, Leads, Traffic) then pick Search, Display, Shopping, or Video based on objective.

3. Keywords & targeting

Start with exact & phrase match for high intent keywords. Add negative keywords to avoid wasted spend.

4. Ad copy & assets

Write compelling headlines, include CTAs, and use responsive search ads for automated combinations.

5. Bidding & budgets

Begin with conservative manual bidding or Target CPA if you have conversion history.

Optimization (Intermediate → Advanced)

Tactics to lower CPA and increase ROI

Ad Extensions

Use sitelink, callout, and structured snippet extensions to increase ad real estate and CTR.

Audience Signals

Layer in remarketing lists, custom intent audiences, and in-market segments for precise targeting.

Search Terms & Negatives

Regularly review search terms to add converting queries as keywords and block irrelevant queries.

Landing Page Fit

Ensure landing pages match ad intent, load fast, and have clear conversion paths.

Experimentation

Run A/B tests on headlines, CTAs, and landing pages. Use drafts & experiments for larger changes.

Smart Bidding

When you have reliable conversion data, test Target CPA or Target ROAS for automated bidding performance.

Pro Tips for Professionals

Advanced practices used by experienced advertisers

SKAGs & Theming

Single-keyword ad groups (SKAGs) or tightly themed ad groups improve relevance and Quality Score.

Data-driven Attribution

Use data-driven or position-based attribution models to understand true conversion touchpoints.

Automation with Rules & Scripts

Automate routine tasks (pause low-performers, adjust bids at scale) using rules and scripts.
