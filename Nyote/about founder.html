About The Founder

Muasembi Mutinda

I'm Musembi Mutinda , the founder of Tera Creations, a digital marketing agency in Kenya that specializes in SEO services. Over the years, I've built a strong personal brand and worked with businesses across different industries — from real estate and logistics to e-commerce, personal brands, and manufacturing companies.

When I started Nyote Creations, my goal was simple: to help businesses get found online. Many Kenyan businesses had beautiful websites, but without SEO they were invisible. That's where I triggered it.

I've always believed that visibility is the foundation of success in the digital age. My journey began by building my own brand online, where I grew a following of over a million across different platforms. That experience taught me how content, SEO, and consistency can change the trajectory of a business.
